07Meta ads, Facebook and Instagram

Ads that are set up to be measured, then to make money.

Most ad accounts waste money because tracking is wrong before the first naira is spent. I fix the foundation first, then build campaigns around what an order is worth to you.

The setup

What I put in place

  • Meta Business Suite and Business Manager, with access given to me as a partner, never your password
  • Ad account, page, Instagram account and payment method connected
  • Meta pixel and Conversions API (CAPI) installed, with purchases counted once and with the right value
  • Domain verification and event priority
  • Product catalogue, if you sell products
  • Tracking tested end to end before launch
The campaigns

How I run them

  • Campaign structure (CBO and Advantage+) matched to your product and budget
  • Audiences, placements, ad copy and creative testing
  • I work out your break-even cost per order before launch
  • Weekly optimisation and a short report you can read
  • You keep ownership of every account and pay Meta directly
Real results from my own accounts

100+ Meta and Google campaigns and 1,000+ orders on a single product in three months. Right now my rechargeable blender campaign has 45 purchases at ₦835 each with an 8.47% click-through rate. The full Ads Manager view is in the "Real campaigns, real purchases" section.

10Questions

Things clients ask me first.

How long does a website take?

A starter site is usually 1 to 2 weeks. A store or booking system takes 2 to 4 weeks. I give you a date in the quote.

Will I own the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting account and code are yours. I never hold your site hostage.

Can I edit it myself afterwards?

Yes. I build it so you can change text, photos and prices yourself, and I show you how.

What if I don't like the result?

You see work in progress at every stage and can ask for changes before the final payment, so there are no big surprises at the end.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide and quote in USD for international projects.

Can you also bring me customers?

Yes. I run Meta and Google ads for online stores, and I can set that up after your site is live.
